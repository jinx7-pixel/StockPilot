# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing levels of experience and background.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a **private** GitHub
message or by using the **Report** button on the offending comment, issue, or
pull request. If the report involves the maintainer directly, use the private
security advisory channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

| Level | What it looks like | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Correction | A private warning, with clarity around the nature of the violation. | Maintainer explains the rule and why it matters. |
| 2. Warning | A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. | Maintainer issues a warning. No interaction with the person involved for a specified period. |
| 3. Temporary ban | A temporary ban from any sort of interaction with the project. | Maintainer bans the person temporarily. |
| 4. Permanent ban | A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction with the project. | Maintainer bans the person permanently. |
